Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Dog Days is a dog care tracking app that lets you log meals, poops, care events, routines, medications, pupdates, and share a Pack with others. You can also set optional notifications and reminders, use Apple Watch quick logging, and invite trusted caregivers or walkers to help with care.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, user ID, display name
  • Pack data: pack name, dog names and profiles, member roles, walker or caregiver access, invite and request codes, caregiver guides, and subscription entitlement status
  • Content you add: meals, poops, care logs, medications, routines, pupdates, photos, notes, and other dog care information
  • Device and session data: push notification tokens, device ID, device name/model, OS version, app version, Apple Watch sync data, and active session information
  • Usage analytics and diagnostics: app events such as sign in, sign up, onboarding, paywall, subscription, log activity, Apple Watch quick logging, invite/request flows, and walker/caregiver activity, along with crash logs, error reports, diagnostic events, device model, OS version, and app version
  • Subscription status: if you purchase a Pack subscription, Apple processes payments and we receive entitlement status needed to unlock Pack features for the Pack

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To sync Pack data across members
  • To show dog care history, caregiver guides, routines, medications, and multi-dog profiles to authorized Pack members, walkers, and temporary caregivers
  • To send optional notifications and reminders
  • To secure accounts and manage active sessions and devices
  • To manage invite codes, join requests, approved caregiver access, and Pack subscription entitlements
  • To understand app usage and improve features
  • To monitor app performance, diagnose crashes and errors, and improve reliability

Analytics and Diagnostics

We use analytics and diagnostic tools, including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, to understand how Dog Days is used, improve features, and identify crashes, errors, and stability issues. This may include app launches, screens viewed, taps and interactions, onboarding and subscription events, crash logs, diagnostic events, device model, operating system version, and app version.

We use this information to operate, improve, and troubleshoot the app. We do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your content is stored privately and is only accessible to you and authorized members of your Pack inside the app. Depending on your Pack settings and approved access, this may include Pack owners, members, walkers, and temporary caregivers. We use trusted service providers to run Dog Days:

  • Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, file storage, messaging, analytics, and crash diagnostics
  • Apple for subscription purchases and entitlement management

These providers process data on our behalf to provide app functionality.

Service Provider Privacy Information

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Your Choices

  • You can delete logs, pupdates, and your account at any time in the app
  • You can leave or delete a Pack, if eligible
  • Pack owners can manage member, walker, and temporary caregiver access
  • You can turn off notifications and reminders

Data Retention

Data is retained until you delete it or your account is deleted. Some data may remain temporarily in backups or logs and is then removed based on provider retention schedules.

Children's Privacy

Dog Days is not directed to children under 13.

Health and Veterinary Information

Dog Days is for tracking and coordinating dog care information. It is not a veterinary, medical, or emergency service. Care logs, medication notes, routines, and reminders are for organization only and should not replace advice from a veterinarian.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted here.
